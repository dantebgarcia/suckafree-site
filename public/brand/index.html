Who we are when we talk

Brand archetypes & voice Fall 2026

Three characters. One crew. Every line we write sounds like the City Kid.

At our fall brand session the crew starred Creator most, then Hero and Every Person, then Caregiver, with a star each for Sage, Lover, Outlaw and Magician. Instead of picking three of Jung’s twelve, we blended the ones we starred into three of our own, each named for something already true about us.

The who

The City Kid

Born here, rides Muni, knows the neighbors, welcomes anyone.

 The what

The Cloud Builder

Dreams it up, then builds it with many hands out of scrap wood and a city bus.

 The why

The Keeper

Holds the space and holds the line: care first, values over norms.

The City Kid

“For the City Kids, as City Kids”

Built from Every Person + Lover, with a Jester streak

Most of our personality list describes this person. They ride Muni, know their neighbor, hike San Bruno and then get Chinese food in Portola, can hang with anyone, and welcome anyone and are welcomed. Queer, working class, Bayview, a magenta mohawk, platform shoes, the friend who hands you a rock as a gift.

Core desire
To belong, and to stay at home in a city that keeps pushing its own kids out.
Gives people
A place where you don’t have to explain yourself.
Watch for
Inside jokes that leave newcomers outside. Nostalgia that turns into “real SF” gatekeeping.
Voice

Warm, local, playful

Like a friend giving you directions to the warehouse: specific, generous, a little funny.

  • Name real places: Islais Creek, 3rd and Quint, the Midway, Heron’s Head, the T.
  • Say “we” and “you.” Keep sentences short.
  • SF words are welcome, as long as a newcomer still gets it.
  • Humor is kind, never at someone’s expense.

Skip: venue-speak (“premier event space”), postcard SF, slang so thick it shuts people out.

Come through. 125 Quint St, right across from Islais Creek. We’ll leave the roll-up door open.
Birthday party, memorial, rave, band practice: if it brings your people together, ask us.

The Cloud Builder

“Many hands, over many years”

Built from Creator + Magician

Creator was the crew’s clear favorite: generative, a new way of doing things, many hands over many years. The proof is everywhere: a retired Muni bus turned rolling gallery, a stage built from scrap wood, a print shop, a brake pad workshop, murals across the warehouse. The Magician half carries the clouds, whales and birds, the Cartoon Network nostalgia, and the feeling that something ordinary can turn special.

Core desire
To make something that wasn’t there before, together.
Gives people
An open invitation to make. No experience needed, tools provided.
Watch for
Always starting, never finishing. Art that forgets the neighborhood. Whimsy that buries the practical info.
Voice

Imaginative, hands-on, inviting

Like an artist pulling you into the project: “grab that end.”

  • Lead with verbs: make, build, print, paint, fix, jam.
  • Dream out loud, then give the practical step: what to bring, what it costs, when.
  • Credit the many hands. Name artists and collaborators.
  • Leave room for wonder, about once per page.

Skip: art-world jargon (“interrogates,” “liminal”), lone-genius framing, “creative vibes” with no next step.

No experience necessary. Tools provided. Bring your hands.
We turned a retired Muni bus into a gallery. Imagine what we can make with you.
SFC fosters interdisciplinary artistic practice.Upstairs, artists paint, print and build side by side. Downstairs, the whole city shows up.

The Keeper

“Holds the space, holds the line”

Built from Caregiver + Hero + Outlaw + Sage

Gives hope, shares what it has, takes responsibility for community, leads with values over norms, tells the truth. In practice: free masks, Narcan and water at every event, air filters, free community use twice a month, Pop Out to the Polls, the resilience hub. “When you get, give. When you learn, teach.” (Maya Angelou)

Core desire
Keep people and place safe, and keep the space in community hands.
Gives people
Steadiness. People trust the space because we keep showing up.
Watch for
Preachiness. Burnout from carrying everything. Aim the fire at systems, never at the person walking in.
Voice

Direct, caring, unbothered

Like the person on the door at a big event: calm, clear, looking out for you.

  • Say the true thing plainly: what we believe, what we need, what we won’t do.
  • Make care concrete: list the access details instead of “we value inclusion.”
  • Name displacement and environmental racism in the Bayview without hedging.
  • Ask for support with confidence, never with guilt.

Skip: grant-speak, guilt-trip fundraising, savior language (“serving the underserved”).

Masks, Narcan and water are free at every event. Take what you need.
Artists across this city are being priced out. We’re building a place that stays.

The three-question test
  1. Would a City Kid say this out loud?
  2. Does it invite someone to make or do something?
  3. Is it honest about what we stand for?

We are not

Draft for the crew
  • A corporate venue or a “premier event space”
  • A nonprofit that “serves” the community. We are the community.
  • A gallery only insiders can walk into
  • Tech-boom SF
  • Too cool to say hi
  • Grim. We push back on displacement and still throw the party.
  • Polished for polish’s sake. Handmade beats slick.

What it looks like

City Kid

Handmade & local

The felt banner as the mark. Muni transfers, Sutro Tower, real photos of real people. Magenta and sky blue as the base.

Cloud Builder

Dreamy & playful

Cloud skies, whales, parrots, sparkles, the green spiral. Collage and sticker layering. Neon green and orange saved for buttons and calls to action.

Keeper

Clear & trustworthy

Plain, very legible body type and generous space wherever info is practical: access, booking, donating. One steady color for care details so they’re always easy to find.
